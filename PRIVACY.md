# Privacy — PDFVEX / PDFix

PDFVEX is the new name for PDFix. Beta 3 still shows PDFix inside the app; the name changes there in Update 3.

Everyday PDF viewing, filling, commenting, and saving can be done on your PC without an account. Downloading the installer from GitHub, visiting a link in your browser, or choosing an online service is separate from that local work.

## Data on your PC

PDFix stores settings, recent file information, and recovery data in your Windows profile. Recovery data can include the text and answers you entered. Saved signatures and document versions can also be kept on the device when you use those features. Review **Settings & privacy** for the controls that apply to your data.

A new copy is a separate file. Check where you save it and review its contents before sharing, especially if you use a folder that syncs to another service.

## Reports and uploads

GitHub issues are public. **Never upload personal, medical, financial, or confidential documents**, even as examples. Use synthetic files. Review screenshots and problem reports before sharing them because they can reveal file names, paths, or other details.

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private vulnerability reporting.
