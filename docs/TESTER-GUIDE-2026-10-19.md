# PDFix test version: a quick guide

Thank you for trying PDFix. This is an early test version, so some things will be rough. Your notes are what
make it better.

## Getting started
1. Your son has put a **PDFix** folder on this computer, with a **PDFix** shortcut on the desktop. Double-click the
   shortcut to open it.
2. The first time, Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is because the test version is not yet
   signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. If PDFix ever does not open, or opens to a blank or flickering window, close it and try once more. If it happens
   again, write down the time and tell your son.

## Your files are safe
- The drawing tools below save a **new PDF copy** and ask where to put it. Keep your original drawing.
- Everything stays **on this computer**. Nothing is uploaded.
- Still, for this test, work on **copies** of important drawings, not the only copy.

## Things to try
- **Open a drawing**: click **Open a file** on the Home screen. Scroll with the mouse wheel; zoom with
  **Ctrl + mouse wheel** while the pointer is over the drawing, or use the **+** and **-** buttons.
- **Find something**: press **Ctrl+F**, type a word or sheet number, press **Enter**. **F3** goes to the next match. Some drawings have no selectable text; use
  **Make a scan searchable** in **All tools** to create a searchable copy first.
- **Mark up a drawing**: **All tools**, search **Mark up a PDF**, then choose your PDF. Click the
  **Note** button and click the drawing, **Arrow** and drag toward the arrow tip, or **Cloud** and drag a box.
  Click **Preview annotated copy**, check it, then **Save annotated copy** with a new name.
- **Measure**: open your drawing, then **Measure > Drawing takeoffs** (or search
  **Drawing takeoffs** in **All tools**).
  Click the two ends of a known length, choose its units and enter **Known reference length**. Tick the check
  and click **Confirm scale**. Click two points for a distance, then **Finish measurement**. Check against a
  dimension you trust; confirm the scale again on another page.
- **Print**: to include new marks, save the annotated copy and **open that saved copy first**. Click **Print**
  above the drawing, choose pages and paper, and wait for the preview. If you change the settings, wait for it to refresh. Check it, tick **I reviewed the
  prepared pages and settings**, then **Open Windows print dialog**. For measured drawings, choose **Actual
  size · 100%** and check the printed scale with a ruler; do not rely on a fitted print for measurements.
- **Fill in and sign a form**: **Fill and sign** on the Home screen.
- **Rearrange pages**: open a PDF and choose **Home > Organize pages**.

If you cannot find something, press **F1** and type what you want to do (for example "rotate" or "print").

## When something goes wrong
Please tell your son. The most useful things are:
1. **What you were trying to do** (for example "adding a cloud to sheet A-201").
2. **What happened instead**, and any message on screen. A photo of the screen with your phone is perfect.
3. **Roughly what time** it happened.
4. **A problem report**: open **Settings & privacy** (bottom left), scroll to **About this version** and click
   **Save a problem report**, or press **F1** and type "problem". Save the file anywhere and send it to your son.
   It lists what PDFix was doing and any error messages. It never includes your drawings, and file and folder
   names are blanked out.

"I couldn't figure out how to..." is just as useful as an error message.

## Closing
Close PDFix with the **X** in the top corner. If you were in the middle of typing a document, PDFix keeps a
recovery copy and offers it back next time.
