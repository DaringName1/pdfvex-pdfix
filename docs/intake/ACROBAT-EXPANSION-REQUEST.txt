Here is the complete, categorized breakdown of everything where **Adobe Acrobat Pro beats PDFix**, based on Adobe’s actual enterprise capabilities and the explicit exclusions, deferrals, and gaps listed in the PDFix catalog:

---

### 1. Legal, Cryptographic & Enterprise Signatures
* **Certified PKI Signatures:** Adobe natively integrates with the **AATL (Adobe Approved Trust List)** and **EUTL (European Union Trust List)**. When you sign in Adobe with a hardware token, SmartCard, or digital certificate, the PDF is cryptographically sealed, tamper-evident, and legally recognized in court under ESIGN, UETA, and eIDAS.
  * *PDFix status:* **Explicitly excluded for initial release.** Only supports visual stamps and drawn/typed visual marks, which have zero cryptographic proof.
* **Cloud Signature Routing & Audit Trails (Adobe Sign):** Adobe offers multi-party signature workflows, automated routing orders, authentication challenges (SMS/email OTP), and legally binding audit logs.
  * *PDFix status:* **Excluded/deferred (F39).** No external signature dispatch, no recipient tracking, and no bulk signature/payment pipelines.

---

### 2. Print-Shop, Prepress & Color Management
* **Commercial Prepress & Preflight:** Adobe includes the industry-standard Preflight engine (Callas Software), capable of automated inspection, color-profile conversion, and syntax verification for commercial printers.
* **Color Trapping & Plate Separation Preview:** Adobe allows commercial printers to inspect individual CMYK and Pantone/spot color plates, overprint simulations, dot gain, and color trapping.
* **Print Standards (PDF/X):** Acrobat Pro can validate and enforce PDF/X-1a, PDF/X-3, PDF/X-4, and PDF/X-6 compliance.
  * *PDFix status:* **Explicitly excluded/deferred (M10.02, F40).** PDFix explicitly excludes advanced print-shop color management/trapping and has deferred PDF/X verification.

---

### 3. Standards Compliance & Archival (PDF/A, PDF/E)
* **Turnkey Archival Conversion (PDF/A):** Adobe can analyze arbitrary PDFs and convert them into compliant ISO standards (PDF/A-1b, PDF/A-2b, PDF/A-3u) with embedded ICC profiles, device-independent colors, and embedded font subsets.
  * *PDFix status:* **Deferred (M10.01, F40).** PDFix treats standards compliance as a future candidate and explicitly notes that neither a visual match nor a file extension guarantees standard compliance.

---

### 4. Accessibility Remediation (Section 508 / PDF/UA / WCAG)
* **Industrial Tag Tree Editing:** Adobe Acrobat Pro contains a full Tag Tree Editor, Reading Order Panel, and Touch Up Reading Order tools required to make PDFs legally compliant for blind/low-vision users.
* **Automated Accessibility Auditing:** Adobe generates detailed compliance reports for PDF/UA and Section 508, allowing bulk artifact tagging, alt-text assignment, and table header mapping.
  * *PDFix status:* **Basic only (M08.01–02, F30).** PDFix only offers basic structural inspection and limited guided repairs; it lacks a full tag-tree remediation environment.

---

### 5. Advanced Forms & Dynamic Scripting
* **Dynamic XFA Forms:** Adobe supports complex, reflowing XML Forms Architecture (XFA) used by federal governments, defense agencies, and healthcare providers.
* **Embedded Acrobat JavaScript Engine:** Adobe executes rich JavaScript inside forms for live validation, arithmetic recalculations, button actions, and formatting.
* **2D Barcode Fields:** Acrobat dynamically generates paper 2D barcodes that encode filled form data as the user types.
* **Database & Web Submissions:** Adobe forms can submit payloads directly to web endpoints or corporate databases.
  * *PDFix status:* **Hard exclusion (M04.01–03, M10.02).** PDFix **refuses to run PDF scripts/macros** for security, does not support XFA forms, and excludes barcode fields entirely.

---

### 6. PDF Portfolios & Container Formats
* **Multi-File PDF Portfolios:** Adobe can bundle dozens of different files (Word, Excel, CAD, video, other PDFs) inside an interactive, navigable 3D or grid-based portfolio package.
  * *PDFix status:* **Explicitly excluded (M10.02).** PDFix cannot create or navigate PDF portfolios.

---

### 7. 3D Models, Geospatial Maps & Multimedia
* **Interactive 3D PDF:** Adobe natively renders embedded 3D CAD models (U3D and PRC formats), allowing users to rotate parts, create cross-section slices, measure 3D geometries, and toggle part assemblies.
* **Geospatial PDF (GIS):** Acrobat recognizes embedded coordinate metadata (latitude/longitude) and allows GIS measurements.
* **Embedded Multimedia:** Acrobat plays embedded audio, video, and rich interactive media.
  * *PDFix status:* **Explicitly excluded (M10.02).** PDFix excludes 3D models, geospatial measurements, and embedded media playback.

---

### 8. Enterprise Rights Management (DRM) & Policy Control
* **Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Document Security:** Organizations can enforce central DRM policies—revoking access to a document remotely even after it has been downloaded, expiring access on specific dates, or restricting printing.
  * *PDFix status:* **Explicitly excluded (M10.02).** Only supports basic local password encryption (open/permission passwords); zero enterprise DRM support.

---

### 9. Multi-Platform Ecosystem & Browser Sharing
* **Cloud Link Reviews (No Software Required):** Adobe allows you to generate a web link where anyone on a Mac, Chromebook, phone, or PC can comment on a PDF in a web browser without installing anything.
* **Mobile Ecosystem & "Liquid Mode":** Adobe has mature iOS/Android apps with reflowing "Liquid Mode" for reading documents on phones.
  * *PDFix status:* **Windows desktop only (F36).** Built strictly for Windows; browser-based shared review and cloud links are deferred candidates.

---

### 10. Engine Maturity on Corrupted/Wild PDFs
* **30 Years of Real-World Parser Tolerances:** Acrobat can reliably open broken, malformed, 1990s PostScript-derived, or non-spec PDFs produced by hundreds of different third-party generators without crashing.
  * *PDFix status:* **Under active development.** A large corpus of adversarial and broken files has not yet been accepted or certified through runtime release gates (F42).
