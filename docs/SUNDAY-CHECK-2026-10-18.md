# Sunday check before Monday (about one hour)

This is the short version of the [everyday checklist](HUMAN-TEST-CHECKLIST.md), aimed at what your dad will do
most. Use the test version Claude names on Sunday (a folder under `releases`; open `app\PDFix.exe`).

Keep a copy of any file you care about before you start. For each item write one line: **what you did, what
happened**, and the file name. "Confusing" or "looked odd" is a useful note. If something goes wrong, take a
screenshot (Windows key + Shift + S) and keep going.

## Starting up (2 minutes)
- [ ] 1. Open PDFix three times (close it in between). Did it open every time? Anything flash, go blank or take
  longer than about 10 seconds? *This checks the graphics problem.*

## Drawings, your dad's main use (25 minutes)
- [ ] 2. Open a real drawing set like the ones your dad uses (the bigger the better). Scroll through, zoom in on
  small text, jump to a page by number. Anything blank, blurry or slow?
- [ ] 3. Press **Ctrl+F** and search for a word or sheet number on the drawings. Did it find it?
  Many drawings are pictures with no searchable text (your dad's sample is one). If nothing is found, open
  **PDF tools > Make scan searchable**, check the recognized words against the drawing (small lettering and
  dimensions especially), save the copy, open it and search again.
- [ ] 4. **Mark up a drawing**: add a note, an arrow and a revision cloud. Save a copy, close it, reopen the copy.
  Are the marks still there and in the right place?
- [ ] 5. Set the scale on a drawing and measure a length you already know. Is the number right?
- [ ] 6. Print one page of a drawing on your printer. Is anything cut off or the wrong size?

## Everyday tools (20 minutes)
- [ ] 7. **Fill and sign**: put your name and today's date on a PDF, save a copy, reopen it.
- [ ] 8. **Fix or combine pages**: turn one page and delete one, save a new PDF, open it.
- [ ] 9. **New document**: write a few lines with a heading and a list, then **Export PDF** and **Export DOCX**.
  Open the Word copy in Microsoft Word. Does it look right?
- [ ] 10. Type in a document, then close PDFix without saving. Open it again: is your work offered back?

## How it looks and reads (10 minutes)
- [ ] 11. Click through Home, Workspaces and a few tools. Write down anything that looks off: words cut off,
  buttons too close together, a message you did not understand (copy its words), a tool whose name does not say
  what it does.
- [ ] 12. Overall: would your dad manage on his own? What would he get stuck on first?

## Your decision for Monday
- [ ] Go: give your dad the test version and his one-page guide.
- [ ] Wait: something above needs fixing first (write which item).

Send your notes back as they are; Claude and Codex will sort them into fixes.
